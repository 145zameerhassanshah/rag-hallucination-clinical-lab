1. **Clinic BP target for adults under 80:** below **140/90 mmHg**.  
   **Supporting sentence:** “For adults with hypertension under age 80, the clinic blood-pressure target is below 140/90 mmHg.”

2. **Clinic BP target for adults aged 80 or over:** below **150/90 mmHg**.  
   **Supporting sentence:** “For adults aged 80 or over, the clinic target is below 150/90 mmHg.”

3. **HBPM/ABPM target for adults under 80:** below **135/85 mmHg**.  
   **Supporting sentence:** “For treatment monitoring using home blood pressure monitoring or ambulatory blood pressure monitoring during usual waking hours, targets are below 135/85 mmHg for adults under 80 and below 145/85 mmHg for adults aged 80 or over.”

4. **HBPM/ABPM target for adults aged 80 or over:** below **145/85 mmHg**.  
   **Supporting sentence:** “For treatment monitoring using home blood pressure monitoring or ambulatory blood pressure monitoring during usual waking hours, targets are below 135/85 mmHg for adults under 80 and below 145/85 mmHg for adults aged 80 or over.”
